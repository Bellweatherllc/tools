How the FBA Matrix works

How it's organized

Bellweather has 8 Core Processes: Leadership, Marketing, Sales, Design, Logistics & Pre-Construction, Project Management, Production, and Operations.

Inside them are 48 Significant Steps. They rarely change, and well-performing staff work from the Significant Steps alone.

Each Significant Step has SOPs underneath it. SOPs can change often, and many are kept for training or reference.

Example
Marketing (Core Process)
Strategy & Budget (Significant Step)
• Brand Identity (SOP)
• “Go To Market” Strategy (SOP)

What FBA means

“Followed By All” means the Significant Steps are understood by everyone who performs them, and the SOPs are documented and reviewed regularly.

The tool tracks the documentation half of FBA: every SOP documented and reviewed on schedule. The people half, where everyone understands and follows the Significant Steps, happens through HODs in L10s, weekly 1:1s, and quarterly conversations. That’s not a box to check; it’s a purpose to fulfill.

A Significant Step reaches FBA in the tool when every SOP under it is documented and its review is Current.

The tabs

The first three tabs are for tracking; the Handbook is the reference document.

FBA
Significant Steps that have reached FBA, with the company-wide count out of 48.
Focus
Significant Steps actively being worked toward FBA.
Remaining
Significant Steps not yet prioritized. Admins use “Move to Focus →” when one is ready to be actively worked.
Handbook
Every Core Process, Significant Step and SOP, with Purpose, seats, and links to SOP documents.

Click any Significant Step to see its SOPs, or use Expand All.

Reading the statuses

Significant Step (FBA Status)

FBA Achieved
Every SOP is documented and Current.
In Progress
Some SOPs are documented, but not all are documented and Current.
Not Started
No SOPs yet, or none documented.

SOP (Review Status)

Current
Documented and reviewed on schedule (or marked as not needing a recurring review).
Due Soon
Next review is within 30 days (7 days for Monthly SOPs).
Overdue
The next review date has passed.
Pending
Not documented yet, or not reviewed for the first time yet.

The Next Review column shows the date the next review is due; N/A means no recurring review is needed.

View by seat

Pick your seat in View by seat to see only your work. The list opens all the way to the SOPs; choose “All seats” to go back to the full, collapsed list.

  • Accountable: one seat per Significant Step. That seat sees the whole Significant Step and all of its SOPs.
  • Responsible: one or more seats per SOP. Those seats see the SOPs they’re named on.

Admin mode

Joey and Melissa can turn on the Admin switch. Changes save immediately to SharePoint.

  • Add, edit or remove Significant Steps and SOPs on the Handbook tab.
  • Set Documented, Last Reviewed, Cadence, Responsible seats, document links and detail bullets on each SOP.
  • Manage the master list of seats on the Seats tab. Renaming a seat updates it everywhere; a seat can only be deleted once nothing is assigned to it.

Something wrong?

Contact Joey or Melissa. If you see a red error message, take a screenshot of it. It contains the details needed to fix the problem.
