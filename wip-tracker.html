How earned income is calculated — reference & sanity check

Design — DA signed, before CA signing
Earned = 50% of whatever's actually been invoiced so far. Design payments run about 25% of the projected construction amount over the phase; only half of each payment is booked as earned, the rest is collected ahead of the work.

Pre-Con — CA signed, before construction starts
Earned = 30% of the (current) contract value — the deposit, paid at CA signing to bring cumulative billing up to 30% of whatever the exact construction amount turned out to be. Flat through Pre-Con: progress stays at 0% until construction's first week.

Construction — the Pipeline's construction phase, start to last week
Earned = 30% of contract (already earned at signing) + 70% of contract × schedule progress (elapsed weeks ÷ construction duration).

A manual override (✎ next to any project's % Complete) always replaces this — it's for the cases the formula can't reach, like a job whose schedule doesn't match reality.
